# Payment Policy

**Company:** NovaShop  
**Version:** 2.2  
**Effective Date:** 2025-01-01  
**Policy Type:** Payment  

---

## Overview

This policy covers accepted payment methods, billing practices, duplicate charge resolution, and payment failure handling at NovaShop.

---

## Accepted Payment Methods

| Method | Supported |
|--------|-----------|
| Visa / Mastercard / Amex | Yes |
| PayPal | Yes |
| Apple Pay / Google Pay | Yes |
| Bank Transfer (ACH) | Yes — orders over $100 |
| Cryptocurrency | No |
| Cash on Delivery | Selected regions only |

---

## Payment Timing

- Payment is **authorized** at the time of order placement.
- Payment is **captured** (charged) when the order is dispatched.
- If an order is cancelled before dispatch, the authorization is released within 1–3 business days and no charge appears on the statement.

---

## Duplicate Charge

A duplicate charge occurs when a customer is charged more than once for a single order. This can happen due to:

- Payment gateway timeout causing a retry.
- Browser refresh during checkout.
- System error.

**Resolution:**
1. The support agent verifies both charge IDs in the payment system.
2. The duplicate is confirmed when two charge records share the same order ID but different transaction IDs.
3. The duplicate amount is refunded within **2 business days**.
4. This is a **P1 (high priority)** case — must be resolved within 24 hours of report.

---

## Failed Payment

If a payment fails at checkout:

- The order is not placed.
- No charge is made.
- The customer is advised to check card details or try another method.

If a payment fails after order placement (deferred capture):

- The customer receives an email notification.
- The order is placed on hold for **48 hours**.
- If payment is not resolved within 48 hours, the order is automatically cancelled.

---

## Shipping Fee Charges

- Shipping fees are charged at checkout with the order total.
- If the order is cancelled before dispatch, the shipping fee is also refunded.
- If Express Delivery is significantly delayed, the shipping fee is refunded per `express_delivery_policy.md`.

---

## Invoice and Receipts

- A payment receipt is emailed automatically after each successful charge.
- Customers can download invoices from their account dashboard.
- For business customers requiring formal invoices, contact support within 30 days of the transaction.

---

## Chargebacks

If a customer initiates a chargeback with their bank without contacting NovaShop first:

- The support team will respond to the bank's inquiry with order and delivery evidence.
- Unresolved chargebacks may result in account review.
- NovaShop recommends contacting support first — most issues are resolved faster through direct contact.

# Damaged and Lost Product Policy

**Company:** NovaShop  
**Version:** 1.8  
**Effective Date:** 2025-01-01  
**Policy Type:** Damage and Loss  

---

## Overview

This policy defines how NovaShop handles reports of damaged products received by customers and packages confirmed as lost in transit.

---

## Damaged Item — Reporting Window

Customers must report a damaged item within **30 days of delivery**. Reports after 30 days are not eligible unless covered by warranty (see `warranty_policy.md`).

---

## Types of Damage

### Shipping Damage

Damage that occurred during transit (visible on the packaging or confirmed by carrier).

**Customer action required:**
- Take photos of the damaged packaging and item.
- Submit via support chat or email within 30 days.

**NovaShop response:**
- Verify carrier tracking and dispatch record.
- Offer a **full replacement** or **full refund** (customer's choice).
- NovaShop covers return shipping cost.
- Resolution within 3 business days of photo submission.

### Manufacturing Defect

A defect that exists at the point of manufacture, not caused by shipping or use.

**Customer action required:**
- Describe the defect and provide photos if possible.
- Report within the return window or warranty period.

**NovaShop response:**
- Offer replacement, repair (for eligible products), or refund.

---

## Lost Package

A package is considered **lost** when:

- Tracking shows no movement for **5 or more consecutive business days**, or
- The carrier confirms the package is lost in an official report.

**Investigation process:**
1. Support agent opens a carrier investigation (typically takes 3–5 business days).
2. If the carrier confirms loss: full refund or replacement is issued immediately.
3. If the carrier cannot confirm: NovaShop escalates internally and issues a resolution within 7 business days.

---

## Wrong Item Delivered

If the customer receives an item they did not order:

- NovaShop arranges pickup at no cost to the customer.
- The correct item is dispatched on priority shipping (Express at no charge).
- If the correct item is out of stock, a full refund is issued.

---

## Customer-Caused Damage

Damage caused by the customer after delivery is not covered under this policy. The customer may still initiate a return if within the return window, subject to the restocking fee and condition requirements in `return_policy.md`.

---

## High-Value Items

For items valued over **$200**, all damage and loss claims require:

- Photo evidence (mandatory).
- Support agent review before a resolution is issued.
- Cases may be escalated to the Damage Review team.

See `escalation_policy.md`.

# Express Delivery Policy

**Company:** NovaShop  
**Version:** 2.1  
**Effective Date:** 2025-01-01  
**Policy Type:** Shipping  

---

## Overview

NovaShop offers Express Delivery as a premium shipping option with a guaranteed delivery window. This policy defines the conditions, timelines, compensation rules, and eligibility criteria for Express Delivery orders.

---

## Delivery Timeframes

| Delivery Type         | Guaranteed Window     |
|-----------------------|-----------------------|
| Express (Domestic)    | 1–2 business days     |
| Express (Regional)    | 2–3 business days     |
| Express (Remote Areas)| 3–4 business days     |

Business days exclude weekends and official public holidays.

---

## Delay Definition

An Express Delivery order is considered **delayed** when:

- The package has not been delivered within the guaranteed window **plus a 1-business-day grace period**, and
- No delivery attempt has been recorded by the carrier.

A delay of **more than 3 business days beyond the guaranteed delivery date** is classified as a **Significant Delay** and triggers automatic compensation eligibility.

---

## Compensation Rules

### Shipping Fee Refund

If the Express Delivery is delayed by **more than 3 business days**, the customer is entitled to a **full refund of the shipping fee** paid at checkout.

- The refund is applied to the original payment method.
- Processing time: 3–5 business days.
- No additional claim is required if the delay is confirmed in the system.

### Order Cancellation

If the package has not been delivered within **7 business days** of the original guaranteed date, the customer may:

1. Request a full order cancellation and refund, or
2. Wait for the delayed delivery and receive a shipping fee refund.

---

## Exceptions — No Compensation

Compensation does not apply when the delay is caused by:

- Incorrect or incomplete delivery address provided by the customer.
- Failed delivery attempts due to no one being present (after 2 attempts).
- Customs clearance for international orders.
- Force majeure events (natural disasters, strikes, government restrictions).
- Customer-requested hold on delivery.

---

## Carrier Responsibility

If the carrier confirms the package is **lost in transit**, the case is escalated to the Damage and Loss team. See `damaged_product_policy.md` for lost package procedures.

---

## How to Request Compensation

Customers do not need to submit a formal claim. Support agents can verify the delay in the order management system and initiate the shipping fee refund directly.

For delays confirmed as Significant Delays, the agent should:

1. Verify order status and carrier tracking.
2. Confirm delay exceeds the 3-business-day threshold.
3. Initiate shipping fee refund via the refund tool.
4. Notify the customer via the response agent.

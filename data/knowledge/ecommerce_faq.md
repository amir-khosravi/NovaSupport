# NovaShop — Frequently Asked Questions

**Company:** NovaShop  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** 2025-01-01  
**Policy Type:** FAQ  

---

## Delivery and Shipping

**Q: How long does standard delivery take?**  
A: Standard delivery takes 5–7 business days. Express delivery takes 1–3 business days.

**Q: My express delivery is late. What can I do?**  
A: If your express delivery is more than 3 business days late, you are entitled to a full refund of the shipping fee. Contact support with your order ID and we will process it.

**Q: Can I change my delivery address after placing an order?**  
A: You can change the address if the order has not yet been dispatched. Once dispatched, we will attempt to redirect the package with the carrier, though redirect fees may apply.

**Q: My tracking hasn't updated in 5 days. Is my package lost?**  
A: If tracking shows no movement for 5 or more business days, we classify this as a potential lost package. Contact support and we will open a carrier investigation within 1 business day.

---

## Returns and Refunds

**Q: How do I return an item?**  
A: Contact support within the return window (15–30 days depending on product type). We will issue a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) code and a prepaid label if eligible.

**Q: When will I receive my refund?**  
A: Refunds to credit/debit cards take 3–5 business days. PayPal refunds take 1–3 business days. Store credit is applied immediately.

**Q: Can I return a used item?**  
A: Yes, but the item must be in acceptable condition. A 10% restocking fee may apply for returned items that are opened but undamaged.

**Q: I received the wrong item. What should I do?**  
A: Contact support immediately. We will arrange free pickup and ship the correct item on priority at no charge.

---

## Payments

**Q: I was charged twice for the same order. What do I do?**  
A: This is treated as a high-priority issue. Contact support with your order ID. We will verify both charge records and refund the duplicate amount within 2 business days.

**Q: My payment failed but I can see a charge on my card. Is that normal?**  
A: The charge you see is likely a temporary authorization hold, not a completed payment. Authorization holds are released within 1–3 business days if the order was not placed successfully.

**Q: Can I pay using two different methods?**  
A: Currently, NovaShop does not support split payment across two methods at checkout. Store credit can be combined with any payment method.

---

## Account and Orders

**Q: How do I cancel an order?**  
A: You can cancel an order from your account dashboard if it has not yet been dispatched. After dispatch, you must wait for delivery and then initiate a return.

**Q: I can't log in to my account. What should I do?**  
A: Use the "Forgot Password" option on the login page. If you still cannot access your account, contact support and we will verify your identity and restore access.

**Q: Where can I find my invoice?**  
A: Invoices are available in your account under Order History. A copy is also emailed after each successful payment.

---

## Damaged and Defective Products

**Q: My item arrived damaged. What do I do?**  
A: Take photos of the damage and contact support within 30 days of delivery. We will offer a full replacement or full refund. Return shipping is covered by NovaShop.

**Q: My product stopped working after 6 months. Is it covered?**  
A: Yes, if the product is within its warranty period (typically 1 year for electronics). Contact support with your order ID and a description of the defect.

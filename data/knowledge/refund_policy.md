# Refund Policy

**Company:** NovaShop  
**Version:** 3.0  
**Effective Date:** 2025-01-01  
**Policy Type:** Refund  

---

## Overview

NovaShop is committed to fair and transparent refunds. This policy defines when a customer is entitled to a full or partial refund, how refunds are processed, and the timelines involved.

---

## Refund Eligibility

### Full Refund — Always Eligible

A customer is entitled to a **full refund** under the following conditions:

| Scenario | Timeframe |
|----------|-----------|
| Order not delivered (confirmed lost) | Anytime after 7 business days past due date |
| Wrong item delivered | Within 30 days of delivery |
| Damaged item delivered | Within 30 days of delivery |
| Order cancelled before dispatch | Anytime before dispatch |
| Duplicate charge detected | Anytime |

### Partial Refund — Eligible Under Conditions

| Scenario | Refund Amount |
|----------|---------------|
| Express delivery significantly delayed (>3 business days) | Shipping fee only |
| Item returned after use (original condition) | Up to 80% of item price |
| Missing item from a multi-item order | Item value only |

### No Refund

A refund is **not** issued when:

- The return window has expired (30 days from delivery).
- The item shows signs of deliberate damage after delivery.
- Digital products have been fully downloaded and activated.
- The item was marked as final sale at purchase.

---

## Refund Processing Time

| Payment Method | Processing Time |
|----------------|-----------------|
| Credit / Debit Card | 3–5 business days |
| PayPal | 1–3 business days |
| Bank Transfer | 5–7 business days |
| Store Credit | Immediate |

---

## How Refunds Are Issued

Refunds are returned to the **original payment method** used at checkout. Exceptions:

- If the original payment method is no longer valid, the refund is issued as **Store Credit**.
- Store Credit does not expire and can be used on any future order.

---

## Duplicate Charges

If a customer was charged more than once for a single order:

1. The support agent must verify both charge records in the payment system.
2. The duplicate charge is refunded within 2 business days.
3. A confirmation email is sent automatically.

This is treated as a **high-priority** case and must be resolved within 24 hours of the complaint.

---

## Refund Escalation

If a refund cannot be processed through standard tools (e.g., payment gateway error, expired card), the case must be escalated to the Finance team within 1 business day.

See `escalation_policy.md` for escalation steps.

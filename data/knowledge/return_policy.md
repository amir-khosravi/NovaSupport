# Return Policy

**Company:** NovaShop  
**Version:** 2.3  
**Effective Date:** 2025-01-01  
**Policy Type:** Return  

---

## Overview

NovaShop accepts returns for most products within a defined window. This policy covers return eligibility, condition requirements, the return process, and special categories.

---

## Return Window

| Product Category | Return Window |
|------------------|---------------|
| Electronics | 15 days from delivery |
| Clothing and Footwear | 30 days from delivery |
| Home and Kitchen | 30 days from delivery |
| Books and Media | 15 days from delivery (unopened only) |
| Perishables / Food | Not returnable |
| Digital Products | Not returnable after activation |
| Personal Care | Not returnable once opened |

---

## Condition Requirements

Items must be returned in the following condition:

- **Original packaging** (box, tags, accessories included).
- **Unused or minimally used** — items showing significant wear are not accepted.
- **Complete** — all parts, manuals, and accessories must be included.

Items that do not meet these conditions will be returned to the customer at their expense, and no refund will be issued.

---

## How to Initiate a Return

1. The customer contacts support within the return window.
2. The support agent verifies eligibility using the order system.
3. A **Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA)** code is issued.
4. The customer ships the item using the provided prepaid label (for eligible returns).
5. Upon receipt and inspection, the refund is processed within 5 business days.

---

## Shipping Costs for Returns

| Return Reason | Who Pays Shipping |
|---------------|------------------|
| Wrong item delivered | NovaShop pays |
| Damaged item delivered | NovaShop pays |
| Customer changed mind | Customer pays |
| Item not as described | NovaShop pays |

---

## Exchange Option

Customers may request an **exchange** instead of a refund for:

- Wrong size or color (clothing/footwear)
- Defective item (within 15 days)

Exchanges are subject to stock availability. If the requested item is unavailable, a refund is issued.

---

## Restocking Fee

A restocking fee of **10% of the item value** applies when:

- The item is returned in opened but undamaged condition.
- The return reason is buyer's remorse (not a product defect).

The fee is deducted from the refund amount.

---

## Non-Returnable Items (Final Sale)

Items marked **Final Sale** at purchase are not eligible for return or exchange, except in cases of manufacturing defect confirmed within 7 days of delivery.

# Shipping Policy

**Company:** NovaShop  
**Version:** 2.0  
**Effective Date:** 2025-01-01  
**Policy Type:** Shipping  

---

## Overview

This policy covers all shipping options available at NovaShop, including standard and express services, tracking, carrier responsibilities, and delivery failure procedures.

---

## Available Shipping Methods

| Method | Estimated Delivery | Cost |
|--------|--------------------|------|
| Standard | 5–7 business days | Free on orders over $50 / $4.99 otherwise |
| Express | 1–3 business days | $12.99 flat rate |
| Same Day (select cities) | Same business day (order by 12:00 PM) | $19.99 |
| International | 10–21 business days | Calculated at checkout |

---

## Order Processing Time

- Orders placed before **3:00 PM local time** on a business day are dispatched the same day.
- Orders placed after 3:00 PM or on weekends are dispatched the next business day.
- During peak seasons (holidays, sales events), processing may take an additional 1–2 business days.

---

## Tracking

All orders include a tracking number sent by email once dispatched. Customers can track their order:

- Via the NovaShop order status page.
- Directly on the carrier's website using the provided tracking number.

Tracking information may take up to 24 hours to update after dispatch.

---

## Delivery Attempts

For packages requiring a signature or for secure delivery:

- The carrier makes **up to 2 delivery attempts**.
- After 2 failed attempts, the package is held at the local depot for **5 business days**.
- If uncollected, the package is returned to NovaShop and a refund is processed minus the return shipping cost.

---

## Address Errors

If the customer provides an incorrect or incomplete address:

- NovaShop will attempt to redirect the package if the carrier allows it.
- Redirect fees are charged to the customer.
- If the package is undeliverable, the return and re-shipping cost is the customer's responsibility.

---

## Package Handover

Once a package is handed to the carrier and a tracking number is assigned, the carrier is responsible for delivery. NovaShop is not liable for:

- Carrier delays beyond our standard processing time.
- Packages lost after confirmed delivery (confirmed by carrier GPS or signature).

For carrier-caused delays, see `express_delivery_policy.md` for compensation rules.

---

## Free Shipping Threshold

- Orders over **$50** qualify for free Standard Shipping automatically.
- Free shipping does not apply to Express or Same Day options.
- Promotional free shipping codes override the threshold requirement.

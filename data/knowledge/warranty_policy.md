# Warranty Policy

**Company:** NovaShop  
**Version:** 1.2  
**Effective Date:** 2025-01-01  
**Policy Type:** Warranty  

---

## Overview

NovaShop provides warranty coverage for eligible products sold on the platform. This policy covers the types of warranty available, claim procedures, and exclusions.

---

## Standard Warranty

All electronics and appliances sold by NovaShop carry a **minimum 1-year warranty** from the date of delivery, covering:

- Manufacturing defects.
- Component failures under normal use.
- Software defects in smart devices (firmware bugs).

---

## Extended Warranty

Customers may purchase an extended warranty at checkout for an additional 1 or 2 years. Extended warranty coverage includes everything in the standard warranty plus:

- Accidental damage (one claim per year).
- Battery replacement for portable devices.

---

## Warranty Claim Process

1. Customer contacts support with order ID and description of the defect.
2. Support agent verifies the purchase date and warranty eligibility in the order system.
3. If within warranty period:
   - Agent arranges pickup for inspection (for items over $100).
   - For items under $100: customer sends a photo; replacement is dispatched if defect is confirmed.
4. Replacement or repair is completed within **7–10 business days**.

---

## Warranty Exclusions

The following are **not covered** by any warranty:

- Physical damage caused by the customer (drops, liquid damage).
- Unauthorized modifications or repairs.
- Normal wear and tear (battery degradation after extended use, cosmetic scratches).
- Damage from power surges (unless surge protection add-on was purchased).
- Products with removed or altered serial numbers.

---

| Product Category | Standard Warranty |

| Smartphones / Tablets | 1 year |

| Home Appliances (large) | 2 years |
| Home Appliances (small) | 1 year |

| Furniture | 1 year (structural defects only) |

---

## Third-Party Seller Warranty

Products sold by third-party sellers on the NovaShop marketplace are covered by the seller's own warranty terms. NovaShop is not directly liable for third-party product defects but will mediate disputes if a seller is unresponsive within 5 business days.
